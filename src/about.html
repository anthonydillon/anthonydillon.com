About me

Anthony Dillon is a Director of Engineering at Canonical, where he leads the Web engineering teams and helps a fast-growing organisation scale its architecture and engineering practices. He's passionate about open source and about building solid design systems that let teams deliver quickly and sustainably, with decisions made close to the work and a clear focus on results. Away from the keyboard, Anthony enjoys running, playing football and spending time with his family.
