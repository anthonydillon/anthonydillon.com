About me

I'm a Director of Engineering at Canonical, where I lead the Web engineering teams and help a fast-growing organisation scale its architecture and engineering practices. I'm passionate about open source and about building solid design systems that let teams deliver quickly and sustainably, with decisions made close to the work and a clear focus on results. Away from the keyboard, I enjoy running, playing football and spending time with my family.
